About

Designer, developer and architect of beautifully simple things, with care.

Comfortable end-to-end: from a WCAG-compliant interface all the way down to Kafka-backed services running on AWS.

I have spent the last eight years inside real production systems: the Prada e-commerce frontend, UniCredit's Dimensione Sociale platform, MSC's Explora Journeys, IoT payment systems, and an AI Chrome extension I co-founded.
