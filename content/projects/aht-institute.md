## Overview

This is the largest of my recent client builds: a full institutional platform for the Africa Hospitality & Tourism Professional Institute. It spans professional-development courses, events, publications and research papers, testimonials, a blog, and a three-tier membership system (individual, group, corporate), plus a career hub with Job Connect, Internship Connect and a Talent Pool.

## Technical choices

**A real content platform, not a brochure site.** The institute publishes continuously (courses, events, research, job posts), so the architecture centers on manageable content types with listing and detail views for each.

**Job applications built in.** Candidates apply directly on the site, uploading a CV and cover letter against specific openings (e.g. hotel positions), which turns the institute into a genuine intermediary between trained talent and hospitality employers.

**Membership onboarding flows.** Individual, group and corporate memberships each have distinct signup paths, since a hotel enrolling 40 staff has very different needs from a solo waiter upskilling.

**Custom domain, production hosting.** Unlike a static demo, this runs on the institute's own infrastructure with a custom domain, SSL and email, delivered as a complete operational handover.

## Outcome

A production platform an African professional body runs its operations on: education, certification, publications and job placement in one system.
