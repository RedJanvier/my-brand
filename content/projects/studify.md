## Overview

Studify is an ed-tech product site and app experience: instant help from tutors, curriculum-aligned study materials by grade level, and personal mentorship. The web experience needed to sell the product to both students and parents, and do it on low-end phones.

## Technical choices

**Mobile-first, performance-first.** Students browse on mid-range Android devices over patchy connections. The build ships a minimal JavaScript payload, system-friendly font loading, and compressed, lazy-loaded imagery.

**Component-driven React.** Feature sections (tutoring, materials, mentorship) are composed from a small set of reusable components, so marketing iterations don't require redesign work.

**Conversion-focused information architecture.** The page answers three questions in order: what is this, why should I trust it, how do I start. Every scroll depth has one call to action.

## Outcome

A crisp product site that communicates the offering in seconds and stays fast on the devices its audience actually owns.
